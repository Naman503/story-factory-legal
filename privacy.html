Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Story Factory is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google API scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly): to upload the owner's videos and read view counts for the owner's own channel.
  • Instagram (Instagram API): to publish the owner's Reels and read their insights.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected.

Story Factory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page or Instagram's "Apps and websites" settings.
